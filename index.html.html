About KrishiMitra

India's Trusted Farming Companion

KrishiMitra is India's leading Smart Agriculture platform serving over 2.4 million farmers across 19 states. Launched in 2019, we have empowered smallholder farmers by bridging the gap between traditional farming practices and modern agricultural science.

According to the India Agriculture Report 2024, farmers using digital advisory platforms see an average 28% increase in crop yield and a 19% reduction in input costs. KrishiMitra is at the forefront of this transformation — offering real-time Mandi prices from 2,700+ APMCs, crop-specific AI advisory, soil health cards, and direct access to PM-KISAN, PMFBY and KCC schemes.

India has 146.45 million hectares of cultivable land and 54.6% of the population engaged in agriculture (Census 2021). KrishiMitra's mission is to make every farmer digitally equipped, financially supported, and market-connected.
